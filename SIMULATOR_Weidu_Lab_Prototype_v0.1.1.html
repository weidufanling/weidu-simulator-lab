    What real evidence would be needed?
    • A coherent, dated baseline with provenance, coverage, measurement uncertainty and permissions.
    • Catchment boundaries, downstream receptors and groundwater connections supported by observations.
    • Process models tested for the intended place, timescale and use, with their shared ancestry recorded.
    • Explicit intervention geometry, engineering, maintenance, failure conditions and alternatives.
    • Checks on transformations, conservation and uncertainty when models exchange information.
    • Local authority and appropriate Traditional Owner governance, including what must remain restricted.
    • Monitoring that can challenge the result, plus clear expiry and refusal conditions.
    This list describes work still needed. No evidence has been collected or scientific review completed for this example.
